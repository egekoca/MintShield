Privacy policy

MintShield minimizes data collection and never requests wallet seeds or private keys.

Data processed

The application processes public wallet addresses, public transaction hashes, signed transaction status and public Coston2, XRPL Testnet and FDC records required to show or execute a testnet intent.

Infrastructure data

Hosting and network providers may process standard request metadata such as IP address, browser information, timestamps and diagnostic logs according to their own policies.

Local preferences

The language preference is stored locally in the browser. MintShield does not currently use advertising or behavioral analytics cookies.

Retention and contact

Public blockchain records cannot be deleted by MintShield. For privacy questions, contact egeyenikale@gmail.com.
